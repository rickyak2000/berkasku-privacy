Privacy Policy for BerkasKu

Last updated: October 2026

BerkasKu ("we", "our", or "app") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our users. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, device permissions, and local processing principles.

1. 100% On-Device Local Processing (No Server Uploads)

BerkasKu is designed with strict privacy-by-design standards:

  • Local Computation: All features—including passport/formal photo background segmentation, suit fitting, signature drawing, PDF merging, document compression, and ATS CV generation—are executed directly on your device.
  • Zero Document Uploads: We do not operate private cloud servers to store, process, or view your national ID cards (KTP), personal photos, resumes, or certificates. Your sensitive documents stay strictly on your smartphone.
  • Local Storage: All generated documents and photos are saved directly into your device's internal/external storage (e.g., Pictures/BerkasKu).

2. Device Permissions & Purpose

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Used solely when you take a photo directly inside the Pas Foto Studio camera guide.
  • Storage / Media: Used solely to allow you to select existing photos or PDFs from your gallery/file manager and save the output.
  • Notifications & Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used exclusively to deliver exam reminders and application deadlines that you explicitly schedule in the Job Tracker feature.

3. Advertising & Analytics

BerkasKu displays banner and interstitial advertisements via Google AdMob:

  • Google AdMob may collect pseudonymous identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID) and crash diagnostic information to serve non-fraudulent, relevant advertisements in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.
  • We do not share any personal biographical data or document contents with AdMob or any third-party advertisers.

4. Target Audience & Children's Privacy

BerkasKu is designed for adults, job seekers, and students aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected on this public webpage.
